## The five
| | Archetype | Their own system | Strengths | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ninja** | The all-rounder: short range, fast, lots of projectiles | Momentum / Boost (speed carries into moves); Substitute (swap to his own shuriken, clone or hook) | Shuriken, Sticky Bomb, Grappling Hook; hovers mid-air with Shuriken / Summon; Backsway; Skull Shaker (a command grab) | Few strong threats from range without momentum; reads punish his high-speed approaches |
| **Cowboy** | Versatile, with long fast sword reach | Limited **bullets** (Quickdraw into Gun stance; bullets ricochet up to 6 times, steered by DI); **Foresight** (an after-image to teleport to, ricochet off, or expand his moveset) | Big disjointed slashes; bullets from strange angles; Teleport super; Lasso | Slow movement; in Gun stance: no defence, no movement, no grab |
| **Wizard** | Setup and zoning | Orb (a companion that keeps attacking); Spark Bombs (slow zone control he can detonate on himself to launch); Missile Form (a fast flight in any direction that can Free Cancel into itself) | Screen control; Hover / fast fall in the air; Telekinesis rocks; combos through Missile Form | Setup-reliant: almost no defence when caught unprepared ("if the enemy catches you with your pants down...") |

## The pattern
Every YOMI character is a plain stick figure with:
1. **one resource or system you can see** (bullets, armour, juke pips, the orb, momentum);
2. **a strong range and a weak one**;
3. **a way to move that's theirs** (Teleport / Foresight, Substitute / Hook, Missile Form, Car Mode, Juke);
4. **a clear limit** that makes the strength fair.

## The Dancer next to them
She's closest to **Wizard** (a setup / zoning spacer) with Cowboy's **limited resource** (two fans) and Ninja's **swap
to a projectile** (Fan Step).
- **Resource:** two fans; ✓ visible in her hands and on her card.
- **Range:** strong at mid range (the fan's edge), weak point blank; ✓ like Wizard and Cowboy.
- **Her own movement:** Fan Step and Silk Glide; ✓ ninja-like. Could grow: Cowboy's Foresight shows how far "go to
  your thing" can carry a kit.
- **Her limit:** no fan, no guard; ✓ like Wizard unprepared.

**Lessons for her:**
- **Steer the fans.** Cowboy's bullets bounce and take DI. Her fans could take the lean on their way back, so the
  return path can be aimed. (Later.)
- **A stall in the air.** Ninja's shuriken stalls him mid-air. Timmy chose "we should fall" (00:09); the two fans
  limit it anyway, so a short stall can come back if wanted.
- **Not armour.** Robot's armour is the opposite of her; she should never trade hits.
- **Make range matter both ways.** Mutant shows a rushdown's answer to a spacer: get in fast. Her answer is the
  universal **Beating Backdash** rule (below) plus her fans in the air.

## Universal mechanics found while researching
- **The prediction colours** (the predictions guide): your ghost is
  - **green** if you get your options first or together;
  - **yellow** if you get them but the opponent goes first;
  - **faded** if limited.

  Over each ghost: "Ready in N" or "Interrupt in N".
- **IASA and Beating Backdash** (the IASA guide, the wiki): backward movement can't be interrupted if the opponent
  uses forward movement, so you're stuck in it until they're actionable. Chasing a retreat pays off.
- **Unblockables:** a grab that beats grabs + a projectile timed for a jump + the corner for a roll.
